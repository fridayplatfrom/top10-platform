## Who each pick suits

### 1. Resend

**Best for** — Developers sending transactional messages from an application.

**Trade-off** — Budget separately if you also need subscriber management and marketing campaigns. [Official source](https://resend.com/pricing)

### 2. Loops

**Best for** — SaaS teams wanting lifecycle campaigns alongside simple newsletters.

**Trade-off** — An account-wide limit may be reached before a particular workflow grows. [Official source](https://loops.so/pricing)

### 3. Customer.io

**Best for** — Product teams triggering journeys from application events.

**Trade-off** — Event taxonomy and profile hygiene take work before automation pays off. [Official source](https://customer.io/pricing)

### 4. Klaviyo

**Best for** — E-commerce teams connecting purchases to segmentation and flows.

**Trade-off** — Email, SMS and other channels can have separate charges. [Official source](https://www.klaviyo.com/pricing)

### 5. beehiiv

**Best for** — Newsletter operators who want publication, email and growth tools together.

**Trade-off** — Check which monetisation and automation features need an upgrade. [Official source](https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing)

### 6. Kit

**Best for** — Creators organising subscribers around tags, products and sequences.

**Trade-off** — Test automation depth and list migration before switching. [Official source](https://kit.com/pricing)

### 7. Postmark

**Best for** — Application teams prioritising transactional delivery and message logs.

**Trade-off** — Design a separate marketing workflow if you need a full campaign suite. [Official source](https://postmarkapp.com/pricing)

### 8. Mailchimp

**Best for** — Small teams needing template-driven campaigns and list management.

**Trade-off** — Review how subscribed and other stored contacts count for billing. [Official source](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)

### 9. Twilio SendGrid

**Best for** — Engineering teams needing API or SMTP email delivery.

**Trade-off** — Buying API capacity may not include the campaign tools marketers expect. [Official source](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/products/email-api/pricing) · [Marketing Campaigns pricing](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/products/marketing-campaigns/pricing)

### 10. Brevo

**Best for** — Teams comparing email and other customer communication channels.

**Trade-off** — Check paid add-ons and whether limits are reported by sends or stored contacts. [Official source](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/)
