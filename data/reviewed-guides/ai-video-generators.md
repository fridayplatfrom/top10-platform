> **Quick answer.** Distinguish generated clips from avatar presenters, editing and dubbing. Price three finished exports, including reruns, instead of comparing only the number of included credits.

## Who each pick suits

### 1. Runway

**Best for** — Editors building short generative shots for a larger production.

**Trade-off** — Gen-3 is a named generation; check which models and export settings are currently supported. [Official source](https://runwayml.com/pricing)

### 2. OpenAI Sora

**Best for** — Former Sora users documenting which products and APIs need a replacement.

**Trade-off** — Old subscription articles cannot give you current access to Sora. Use the official closure notice to check what ended and when. [Official source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation)

### 3. Kling AI

**Best for** — Creators comparing text- and image-to-video motion.

**Trade-off** — Use one reference frame to see how many reruns a usable shot requires. [Official source](https://klingai.com/)

### 4. HeyGen

**Best for** — Teams producing avatar-led introductions or multilingual presenter videos.

**Trade-off** — Verify minutes, custom avatar eligibility and commercial consent requirements. [Official source](https://www.heygen.com/pricing)

### 5. Luma Dream Machine

**Best for** — Creators experimenting with short shots from text or an image.

**Trade-off** — Generation settings and multiple attempts can consume the monthly allowance. [Official source](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine/pricing)

### 6. Synthesia

**Best for** — Workplaces making scripted training or internal explainers.

**Trade-off** — A presenter workflow is less suited to open-ended cinematic scene generation. [Official source](https://www.synthesia.io/pricing)

### 7. Descript

**Best for** — Teams editing interviews, podcasts or existing recordings through transcripts.

**Trade-off** — Check transcript and AI limits rather than treating it as a text-to-video engine. [Official source](https://www.descript.com/pricing)

### 8. Pika

**Best for** — Social-video creators testing short visual effects and transformations.

**Trade-off** — Pika 2.0 is a version label; verify which effects your tier includes today. [Official source](https://pika.art/pricing)

### 9. ElevenLabs Dubbing

**Best for** — Teams localising existing audio and video for another language.

**Trade-off** — Translation, timing and pronunciation still need human review. [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing)

### 10. CapCut Commerce Pro

**Best for** — Retailers assembling product-led vertical videos and social ads.

**Trade-off** — Check that your market supports the advertised workflow and asset rights. [Official source](https://www.capcut.com/)
