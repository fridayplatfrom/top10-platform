> **Quick answer.** Trial the same real project with a guest collaborator and a monthly report. The cheapest plan is only comparable if it supports the roles and workflow you actually need.

## Who each pick suits

### 1. Linear

**Best for** — Product engineering teams planning work around issues and cycles.

**Trade-off** — Check guest access and cross-team reporting needs before standardising. [Official source](https://linear.app/pricing)

### 2. Notion Projects

**Best for** — Teams wanting project notes and databases in one workspace.

**Trade-off** — Powerful customisation means someone has to maintain shared conventions. [Official source](https://www.notion.com/pricing)

### 3. Jira

**Best for** — Software teams needing structured issue workflows and permissions.

**Trade-off** — Price apps and administration along with the cloud subscription. [Official source](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/pricing)

### 4. Asana

**Best for** — Cross-functional teams coordinating tasks and dependencies.

**Trade-off** — Testing one project will not reveal whether portfolio reporting needs a higher tier. [Official source](https://asana.com/pricing)

### 5. ClickUp

**Best for** — Teams that want tasks, docs and multiple views in one tool.

**Trade-off** — Set up one agreed workflow first to avoid redundant statuses and views. [Official source](https://clickup.com/pricing)

### 6. Plane

**Best for** — Issue-tracking teams weighing self-hosting or a managed deployment.

**Trade-off** — Check which features are available in your hosting option and who owns updates. [Official source](https://plane.so/pricing)

### 7. Monday.com

**Best for** — Operations teams that want editable workflow boards.

**Trade-off** — Ask about minimum billable seats and quotas on rules and integrations. [Official source](https://monday.com/pricing)

### 8. Shortcut

**Best for** — Software teams that organise around stories, epics and iterations.

**Trade-off** — Check external collaborator permissions and how work connects to your repo. [Official source](https://www.shortcut.com/pricing)

### 9. Basecamp

**Best for** — Small teams that want a straightforward place for messages, tasks and files.

**Trade-off** — Check if the simpler workflows cover your reporting and dependency needs. [Official source](https://basecamp.com/pricing)

### 10. Trello

**Best for** — Small teams organizing work on a shared board.

**Trade-off** — Cross-project reporting and portfolio planning may need a higher tier or another tool. [Official source](https://trello.com/en/pricing)
