## Who each pick suits

### 1. Attio

**Best for** — Teams that want a flexible contact and company data model.

**Trade-off** — Customising the workspace takes ownership and clear data rules. [Official source](https://attio.com/pricing)

### 2. HubSpot Smart CRM

**Best for** — Teams combining sales, marketing and service in one system.

**Trade-off** — Hub, seat and contact costs may rise independently. [Official source](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/crm)

### 3. Folk CRM

**Best for** — Small teams doing relationship-led outreach.

**Trade-off** — Check what bulk outreach and integration limits apply before importing contacts. [Official source](https://www.folk.app/pricing)

### 4. Salesforce Sales Cloud

**Best for** — Organizations needing a configurable sales process and enterprise controls.

**Trade-off** — Allocate setup and ongoing administration time. [Official source](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/)

### 5. Pipedrive

**Best for** — Small sales teams that want a deal-focused visual pipeline.

**Trade-off** — Confirm automation and reporting are included in your chosen tier. [Official source](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing)

### 6. Close CRM

**Best for** — Outbound sales teams that want email and calling around the lead record.

**Trade-off** — Usage or numbers can add costs beyond the base plan. [Official source](https://www.close.com/pricing)

### 7. Zoho CRM Plus

**Best for** — Teams seeking several CRM and customer-engagement apps under one contract.

**Trade-off** — Compare against standalone Zoho CRM if the team will use only sales features. [Official source](https://www.zoho.com/crm/crmplus/pricing.html)

### 8. Freshsales Suite

**Best for** — Smaller teams considering sales tools with marketing automation.

**Trade-off** — Check contact limits and what communications credits cost at volume. [Official source](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/suite/pricing/)

### 9. Monday Sales CRM

**Best for** — Teams that prefer configuring work from visual boards.

**Trade-off** — Ask how many seats must be bought at your headcount. [Official source](https://monday.com/crm/pricing)

### 10. Copper CRM

**Best for** — Google Workspace teams that want CRM workflows close to email and calendar.

**Trade-off** — Check whether required integrations and automation need a higher tier. [Official source](https://www.copper.com/pricing)
