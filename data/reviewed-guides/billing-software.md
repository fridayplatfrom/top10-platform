## Who each pick suits

### 1. Paddle Billing

**Best for** — Digital product sellers seeking a merchant of record with tax handling.

**Trade-off** — Verify supported products and countries; a flat transaction fee weighs more on small purchases. [Official source](https://www.paddle.com/pricing)

### 2. Stripe Billing

**Best for** — Teams already on Stripe that need subscriptions, invoices and usage billing.

**Trade-off** — Price Billing, Payments and Tax independently for your market. [Official source](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing)

### 3. Chargebee

**Best for** — Subscription businesses managing changes, renewals and dunning across payment gateways.

**Trade-off** — Check overages and whether revenue recognition is included. [Official source](https://www.chargebee.com/pricing/)

### 4. Lemon Squeezy

**Best for** — Independent software sellers who want hosted checkout and merchant-of-record services.

**Trade-off** — Check supported geographies, product rules and the fee treatment of international transactions. [Official source](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing)

### 5. Orb Billing

**Best for** — Usage-priced products that need to turn event streams into invoices.

**Trade-off** — Usage events need stable identifiers and a reconciliation plan before migration. [Official source](https://www.withorb.com/pricing)

### 6. Zoho Billing

**Best for** — Teams already using Zoho that want recurring invoicing and customer self-service.

**Trade-off** — Compare limits and payment gateways for your edition. [Official source](https://www.zoho.com/billing/pricing/)

### 7. Recurly

**Best for** — Subscription companies evaluating renewal recovery and gateway flexibility.

**Trade-off** — Ask how gateway fees, volumes and migration are treated in the proposal. [Official source](https://recurly.com/pricing/)

### 8. Maxio

**Best for** — B2B SaaS finance teams tying subscriptions to reporting and revenue workflows.

**Trade-off** — Run a sample invoice and accounting export before moving customer contracts. [Official source](https://www.maxio.com/pricing)

### 9. Lago

**Best for** — Engineers who want ownership of how usage events become billing records.

**Trade-off** — Self-hosting transfers uptime and billing operations to your team. [Official source](https://getlago.com/pricing)

### 10. Zuora

**Best for** — Enterprises with multiple product lines and complex contract changes.

**Trade-off** — Ask for year-one implementation cost as well as the ongoing software contract. [Official source](https://www.zuora.com/products/billing/)
