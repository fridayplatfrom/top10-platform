## Who each pick suits

### 1. Sonos Era 300

**Best for** — Listeners with a Sonos system who want supported spatial-audio playback.

**Trade-off** — Check which services and setups actually deliver spatial audio. [Official source](https://www.sonos.com/en-us/shop/era-300)

### 2. KEF LS50 Wireless II

**Best for** — Buyers wanting powered stereo speakers without a separate amplifier.

**Trade-off** — Measure placement space and price stands or a subwoofer separately. [Official source](https://us.kef.com/products/ls50-wireless-2)

### 3. Genelec 8030C

**Best for** — Editors and mixers who want a compact powered nearfield reference.

**Trade-off** — Budget for two units, interfaces and room setup; this is studio equipment. [Official source](https://www.genelec.com/8030c)

### 4. Bose Smart Ultra Soundbar

**Best for** — TV owners who want a simple soundbar for dialogue and films.

**Trade-off** — A soundbar is a different tool from a pair of reference monitors. [Official source](https://www.bose.com/pxp/bose-smart-ultra-soundbar)

### 5. JBL Authentics 500

**Best for** — Listeners wanting an all-in-one speaker for a shared room.

**Trade-off** — Its single enclosure does not replace two independently positioned studio speakers. [Official source](https://www.jbl.com/AUTHENTICS-500.html)

### 6. Audioengine A5+ Wireless

**Best for** — Desk listeners who want a compact stereo setup.

**Trade-off** — Check input connectivity and measure the footprint on the desk. [Official source](https://audioengine.com/)

### 7. Devialet Phantom I 108 dB

**Best for** — Buyers looking for a premium single-enclosure listening setup.

**Trade-off** — A stereo setup may require a second unit; confirm compatibility before buying older stock. [Official source](https://www.devialet.com/en-us/product/2XMM615/)

### 8. Klipsch The Fives

**Best for** — People connecting a compact powered stereo system to their TV.

**Trade-off** — Compare placement and input options before buying for a small desk. [Official source](https://www.klipsch.com/uk/products/the-fives-powered-speakers)

### 9. Edifier S3000Pro

**Best for** — Listeners wanting a self-contained powered stereo pair.

**Trade-off** — Confirm the remote, input options and pair contents for the exact listing. [Official source](https://www.edifier.com/int/us/p/bookshelf-speakers/s3000-pro)

### 10. Bang & Olufsen Beosound A5

**Best for** — Listeners who want a portable premium speaker for shared spaces.

**Trade-off** — A portable all-in-one fills a different need from a fixed studio pair. [Official source](https://www.bang-olufsen.com/)
