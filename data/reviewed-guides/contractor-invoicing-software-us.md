> **Quick answer.** Price your real workflow: a deposit, a change order and a final invoice. Ask what payments, extra staff and support add to the amount you pay.

## How we compared

We used published vendor plans, product pages, support documentation and official storefronts. Where we could verify a price, we noted the applicable plan, market or model. Quote-only, variable and older listings have pricing notes that explain what to check.

A solo tradesperson can start with a Joist estimate and turn it into an invoice. If you dispatch a crew, run the same job through Jobber. If you invoice by construction milestone, ask Buildertrend to show the whole process from change order to payment.

## Who each pick suits

### 1. Jobber

**Best for** — Home-service crews that need scheduling and invoicing in one workflow.

**Trade-off** — Check payment fees and whether the tier covers the number of field users. [Official source](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)

### 2. Joist

**Best for** — Solo operators who quote from a phone or tablet.

**Trade-off** — Check limits, payment fees and integrations on the chosen tier. [Official source](https://www.joist.com/pricing/)

### 3. Housecall Pro

**Best for** — Service businesses coordinating dispatch and customer communications.

**Trade-off** — Ask which reporting, payment and marketing features are add-ons. [Official source](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)

### 4. Square Invoices

**Best for** — Small contractors already accepting payments through Square.

**Trade-off** — A free invoice plan still incurs fees when customers pay electronically. [Official source](https://squareup.com/us/en/invoices/pricing)

### 5. QuickBooks Online

**Best for** — Contractors already doing bookkeeping in QuickBooks.

**Trade-off** — Compare ongoing plan price and check when job-costing features become available. [Official source](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/online/)

### 6. FreshBooks

**Best for** — Small contractors invoicing for time and expenses.

**Trade-off** — Active client limits can prompt an upgrade even with one user. [Official source](https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing)

### 7. Zoho Invoice

**Best for** — Small operators needing straightforward estimates and invoices.

**Trade-off** — Compare usage limits and whether a separate accounting or dispatch app is needed. [Official source](https://www.zoho.com/us/invoice/pricing/)

### 8. Wave

**Best for** — Very small businesses that want invoices in the same system as basic bookkeeping.

**Trade-off** — Verify whether automation and payment terms apply to the selected country and plan. [Official source](https://www.waveapps.com/pricing)

### 9. Buildertrend

**Best for** — Remodelers and builders who need project management as well as billing.

**Trade-off** — Ask to see progress billing and change orders on your own contract example. [Official source](https://buildertrend.com/pricing/)

### 10. ServiceTitan

**Best for** — Larger trade businesses considering dispatch, field service and invoicing together.

**Trade-off** — Ask for a written total with onboarding, seat or technician assumptions and add-ons. [Official source](https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing)
