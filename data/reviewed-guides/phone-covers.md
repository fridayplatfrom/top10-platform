## Who each pick suits

### 1. CASETiFY Impact Case

**Best for** — Buyers who want to choose a design with everyday case protection.

**Trade-off** — Printed and licensed designs may cost more; verify magnetic accessory support. [Official source](https://www.casetify.com/)

### 2. dbrand Grip Case

**Best for** — People prioritising textured grip and custom appearance.

**Trade-off** — Check whether your configuration includes a skin and which accessories fit. [Official source](https://dbrand.com/shop/grip)

### 3. Spigen Ultra Hybrid MagFit

**Best for** — People who want a clear case with a magnetic accessory ring.

**Trade-off** — The clear finish may show wear and colour changes differently depending on material. [Official source](https://www.spigen.com/)

### 4. Nomad Modern Leather Case

**Best for** — Buyers who want leather that changes appearance with use.

**Trade-off** — Check the cutouts and magnetic accessory fit for your phone’s exact model. [Official source](https://checkout.nomadgoods.com/products/modern-leather-case-brown-iphone-17)

### 5. Peak Design Everyday Case

**Best for** — People who specifically want Peak Design’s mounting system.

**Trade-off** — A mount plus case costs more than buying a case on its own. [Official source](https://www.peakdesign.com/global/collections/phone-cases)

### 6. Caudabe Sheath

**Best for** — People looking for textured grip in a minimally styled case.

**Trade-off** — Choose the right camera-button and magnetic variant where offered. [Official source](https://caudabe.com/products/sheath-iphone-17)

### 7. Mous Limitless

**Best for** — Buyers comparing a protective magnetic case with a distinct finish.

**Trade-off** — Accessories can change total cost; verify device-specific compatibility. [Official source](https://www.mous.co/)

### 8. OtterBox Defender Series XT

**Best for** — People who are willing to carry a larger case for everyday protection.

**Trade-off** — Check pocket fit, screen protector clearance and included accessories. [Official source](https://www.otterbox.com/collections/defender-xt)

### 9. PITAKA MagEZ Case 5

**Best for** — People who want a slim aramid-fibre case compatible with their magnetic setup.

**Trade-off** — Check corner coverage and camera lip height if drops are your main concern. [Official source](https://www.ipitaka.com/products/awa-magez-case-5)

### 10. Bellroy Leather Case

**Best for** — Buyers who prefer a simple leather look without a bulky case.

**Trade-off** — Check which models include magnets and expect the leather finish to age with use. [Official source](https://bellroy.com/)
