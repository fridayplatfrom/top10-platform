## Who each pick suits

### 1. Genshin Impact

**Best for** — Players who want an open-world action RPG and long-running events.

**Trade-off** — Needs storage, ongoing updates and an internet connection; spending is optional. [Official source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/genshin-impact/id1517783697)

### 2. Balatro Mobile

**Best for** — Players who prefer a solo card-based strategy game and a one-off purchase.

**Trade-off** — Check whether you are viewing the paid app or a subscription-catalogue version. [Official source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/balatro/id6502453075)

### 3. Brawl Stars

**Best for** — Players wanting brief multiplayer matches on a phone.

**Trade-off** — Online competition and progression rewards make it a different fit from an offline game. [Official source](https://supercell.com/en/games/brawlstars/)

### 4. Honkai: Star Rail

**Best for** — Players who prefer a turn-based RPG and story chapters.

**Trade-off** — Characters and progression can involve limited-time events; check your storage requirements. [Official source](https://hsr.hoyoverse.com/)

### 5. PUBG Mobile

**Best for** — Players wanting online squad battle royale in a supported market.

**Trade-off** — Store availability differs by country; check the official listing for your region. [Official source](https://www.pubgmobile.com/)

### 6. Clash Royale

**Best for** — Players who want short online strategy matches around deck building.

**Trade-off** — Account progression and season changes affect the experience over time. [Official source](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/)

### 7. Call of Duty: Mobile

**Best for** — Players looking for mobile multiplayer shooting and familiar modes.

**Trade-off** — Storage needs, regional publisher and update schedules may vary. [Official source](https://www.callofduty.com/mobile)

### 8. Marvel Snap

**Best for** — Card-game players who want short matches and compact decks.

**Trade-off** — Regional availability and the approach to collecting cards matter more than the download price. [Official source](https://www.marvelsnap.com/)

### 9. Pokémon TCG Pocket

**Best for** — Players who want digital card collecting and lighter battles.

**Trade-off** — Check pack timing and whether you prefer the full tabletop rules instead. [Official source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pok%C3%A9mon-tcg-pocket/id6479970832)

### 10. Roblox Mobile

**Best for** — People looking for a wide range of community-made experiences.

**Trade-off** — Individual experiences vary. Review spending and account controls for a child’s account. [Official source](https://www.roblox.com/)
